# Create: Mob Grinding 1.5.0

## New Features
* **Stackable Spawner Chunks**: a Rotational Mob Spawner can hold up to 8 chunks of the same mob. Each chunk spawns one more mob per cycle, so 8 chunks work like 8 spawners in a single block, at a slightly lower stress cost. Right-click with a matching chunk to add it, use a Wrench to take them all back.
* **Automation Support**: Funnels, Hoppers and Mechanical Arms can insert and extract Spawner Chunks. Only chunks of the mob already inside are accepted, up to the stacking limit.
* **Comparator Output**: the spawner outputs its progress towards the next spawn, the grinder how full its Vacuum inventory is.
* **Vacuum Filter**: the Vacuum Upgrade unlocks a Create filter slot on top of the grinder. Collected items that don't match the filter (List Filter in whitelist or blacklist mode, Attribute Filter or a single item) are voided instead of stored. Experience Nuggets are always kept.
* **Grinder Kill Counter**: the grinder counts its kills, shown in Jade and through a Display Link.
* **Wandering Trader Trade**: the Wandering Trader can offer a Scrap Spawner Chunk for 32 Emeralds (rare offer, single use).

## Ponder
* New scenes: Stacking Spawner Chunks, Automating the Spawner, Spawner Chunk Bag, Fan Filter Upgrade (also shown on Create's Encased Fan) and Monitoring the Grinder.
* All scenes are now split into keyframes, so you can jump between steps.

## Config
* The config has been reorganized into sections: spawner, chunk stacking, grinder, soul extractor, enchantments and trading.
* Previously hardcoded values are now configurable: spawner stress per tier, mob cap and its radius, light levels, blade damage and stress, attack speed, soul extraction health threshold, pet protection, Beheading chances and the Wandering Trader trade.
* Existing options keep their names, so your custom values are preserved when updating.
* Fixed the spawn time comments: the old examples were off by 20x (2000 points is about 1.6 seconds at 64 RPM, not 31).

## Changes
* Display Link sources now refresh on their own: spawn progress 4 times per second, grinder kills twice per second, spawner mob once per second.

## Bug Fixes
* Fixed the Rotational Mob Grinder facing the wrong way when placed next to a shaft, leaving the shaft disconnected.
