## Bug Fixes
* Fixed the Rotational Mob Spawner spawning mobs outside its indicated area, on top of itself or inside shafts.
* The No Conditions Upgrade no longer lets mobs spawn inside blocks.
* Fixed Iron Grinder Blades being duplicated by breaking and placing the Grinder.
* Sneak + Wrench now puts the Spawner and Grinder in your inventory, like Create blocks.
* Blank Spawner Chunks now appear in dungeon, mineshaft, stronghold and ancient city chests (they never did before).

## Changes
* A broken Grinder keeps its blade: the item shows the blade color and lists its enchantments while holding Shift.

## Config
* `spawnRange` is replaced by `spawnRadius` (default 1, a 3x3x3 area), and the wrench indicator always shows the real spawn area.
